Stays

Aug 19 · Fairbanks · Chena River Room

Pike's Waterfront Lodge

On the Chena River near FAI. Free 24/7 airport and train-depot shuttle, breakfast buffet, and Pike's Landing next door for dinner.

Aug 20–22 · Denali · Standard Room

Grande Denali Lodge

On Sugarloaf Mountain a mile north of the park entrance. Alpenglow Restaurant. A courtesy shuttle runs to the depot and the visitor center.

Aug 22 · Anchorage · Deluxe Room

Hotel Captain Cook

Historic Hotels of America member, three towers, inlet or mountain views. Home of the Crow's Nest restaurant on the top floor.

Aug 23 · Seward · Harbor View

Harbor 360 Hotel

On the Small Boat Harbor, a block from the depot and close to J-Dock on 4th Ave. Pool, free shuttle, continental breakfast.

Aug 24–26 · Kenai Fjords · 2 nights

Kenai Fjords Glacier Lodge

Alaska Wildland Adventures. 16 log cabins facing Pedersen Glacier inside the national park. Boat in and out from Seward; meals and guided activities included.

Aug 26–28 · Anchorage · Standard Room

The Wildbirch Hotel

JdV by Hyatt, opened 2025, at 239 W 4th Ave in the Mushing District. Walkable to the depot, the museum and the food tour. Crimson restaurant.
