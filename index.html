Stays

Aug 19 · to book

Pike's Waterfront Lodge

On the Chena River near FAI. Free 24/7 airport and train-depot shuttle, breakfast buffet, and Pike's Landing next door for dinner.

Ask the van to run you to the 8:20 AM train.

Aug 20–22 · Denali

Grande Denali Lodge

On Sugarloaf Mountain a mile north of the park entrance. Rooms are about 300 sq ft. Alpenglow Restaurant. A courtesy shuttle runs to the depot and the visitor center.

Book a Grande Cabin, or at least a guaranteed canyon view (published 2026 peak rates: standard $459, view $509).

Aug 22 · Anchorage

Hotel Captain Cook

Historic Hotels of America and Preferred Hotels member, three towers, every room with an inlet or mountain view. Home of the Crow's Nest.

Crow's Nest Suite: one or two bedrooms plus a parlor, secured floor, Captain's Deck lounge.

Aug 23 · Seward

Harbor 360 Hotel

96 rooms on the Small Boat Harbor, a block from the depot. Seward's only hotel pool. Free shuttle; continental breakfast.

Deluxe King Harbor Balcony room: private balcony over the harbor.

Aug 24–26 · Kenai Fjords

Kenai Fjords Glacier Lodge

Alaska Wildland Adventures. 16 log cabins with private baths on a Native-owned 1,700-acre sanctuary, facing Pedersen Glacier. Boat in and out from Seward; meals and guided activities included.

Add the lodge's Seward-to-Anchorage train transfer on the way out.

Aug 26–28 · Anchorage

The Wildbirch Hotel

Opened June 2025 as Anchorage's first lifestyle boutique hotel and the first JdV by Hyatt in Alaska. 252 rooms at 239 W 4th Ave in the Mushing District, walkable to the depot and museum. Crimson restaurant; harbor-view kings.

Verdict: good, modern, and new, but OTAs rate it a tier below the Captain Cook. For "top option in each," return to the Captain Cook. Keep Wildbirch if you'd rather try the new hotel; book a harbor-view room.
