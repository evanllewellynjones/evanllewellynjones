Jones Assets

jonesassets.netlify.app · password-protected

Private family finance dashboard. Monthly financial commentary and household spending reports are written in two Claude Projects, then published here as HTML pages.

Repo
None, on purpose. Files are dropped straight into Netlify so nothing sits in a GitHub repo.
Access
Netlify password. No passwords or account numbers on this site.
Commentary
Monthly Financial Commentaries (Claude Project). Each month:
  1. Inputs. One Bloomberg portfolio PDF with month-end prices and contribution; check that MTD is correct. Last month's commentaries are uploaded as the template.
  2. Build. Python with python-docx and helpers.py builds four separate .docx files.
  3. Review. Evan reviews the .docx files and creates the final PDFs.
  4. Publish. PDFs go in one folder per family member. Add the month to trusts.html (top of each investments array) and investment_checking.html (JONES grid), bump the Updated dates, and drag the whole folder onto Netlify.
  5. Send. Evan emails the family with a link to Jones Assets.
Spending
Jones Spending (Claude Project). Each month the updated workbook goes in, with the 2026 Expenses tab filled through the month just closed. Two outputs:
  1. Dashboard. build.py reruns on the whole 2026 tab, because earlier months get adjusted, and rebuilds 2026_spending_review.html. It ships only when the four reconciliation checks pass and every category × month matches workbook columns K–V.
  2. Commentary. report.py pulls the six personal categories plus Subscriptions and Subscription-annual. Claude writes the notes into highlights_<YYYY-MM>.json, and gen.js turns them into Spending_Commentary_<Month>_2026.docx, which is rendered to PDF and checked page by page.

Deploy. The dashboard HTML goes to Netlify only, never GitHub. Once the PDF is saved, its exact filename goes back into the dashboard so the commentary link points to the right file.
